Why this camp

Silicon Valley immersion + Growing IQ pedagogy

Students don’t just tour tech campuses — they practice the critical thinking and data skills that matter in an AI-driven world, then apply them in real conversations and presentations.

SV

Experience the cutting edge

The Bay Area is the global tech hub. Through site visits, guest speakers, and hands-on experiences (autonomous driving demos, factory tours, campus walks), students gain firsthand insight into the AI and technology shaping their future.

IQ

Learn data science the GIQ way

Growing IQ’s Silicon Valley–style program uses relatable themes — sports and food-tracking businesses — to strengthen critical thinking. Emphasis on reasoning over “right answers,” confidence, and a growth mindset.

🌏

Asia peer cohort

Connect with students from Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and beyond. Living and learning together builds international communication skills and a truly global perspective.

🎤

Communicate with confidence

Dedicated communications and public speaking classes, plus a final presentation, help students turn insights into clear, confident delivery — essential for any future path.

Curriculum

Three pillars: data, business, communication

English-medium instruction. Conversational English expected — open to a wide audience, not marketed as ESL-only.

Growing IQ

Sports Data Science

Analyze real sports scenarios with data. Students practice collecting signals, spotting patterns, and defending conclusions — building AI-era critical thinking through themes they care about.

Growing IQ

Money Matters

Business simulation and money science classes that connect data skills to entrepreneurship — from food-tracking style ventures to how decisions look when numbers meet real stakes.

Communications

Public Speaking

Structured communications classes and a final presentation & graduation showcase. Students leave better able to explain ideas clearly to peers and adults.

What ages / grades can join?
Grade 7 to Grade 10 (approximately ages 12–16). Students should be physically and mentally ready for a dorm-based group program and willing to communicate actively with peers.
Does my child need perfect English? Is this an ESL camp?
This is an English-medium program for an open audience — not ESL-only. Students learn data, money, and communication skills in English, with teaching that invites participation (including a “no wrong answer” classroom culture from past camps). Strong fit for bilingual and international-school families.

英語イマージョン（ESL専門コースではありません）｜対象：中1–高1相当（Grade 7–10）

Is the program safe for middle/high school travelers?
Operations run under the SKYUS × Growing IQ partnership. In the 2026 sample year, students met and dismissed at SFO and studied on the CSUEB campus. Full 2027 staffing and protocol details are shared after early-interest inquiry once dates lock (TBA Nov/Dec). Parents receive packing and day-to-day guidance before travel.
