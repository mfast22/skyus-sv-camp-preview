Why this camp

Silicon Valley immersion + Growing IQ pedagogy

Students don’t just tour tech campuses — they practice the critical thinking and data skills that matter in an AI-driven world, then apply them in real conversations and presentations.

IQ

Learn data science the GIQ way

Growing IQ’s Silicon Valley–style program uses relatable themes — sports and food-tracking businesses — to strengthen critical thinking. Emphasis on reasoning over “right answers,” confidence, and a growth mindset.

FAQ

Common questions

When are Summer 2027 dates?
Exact dates are TBA. The team is locking price and scope now; the full schedule is planned for announcement in November/December, with formal promotion from January. Register early interest below so we can update you first.
What ages / grades can join?
Grade 7 to Grade 10 (approximately ages 12–16). Students should be physically and mentally ready for a dorm-based group program and willing to communicate actively with peers.
Is this an ESL camp?
English-medium, open audience — not an ESL-only product. Conversational English and willingness to engage are expected. Language support is built into tasks within the support we can actually staff — we’ll be honest if another programme is a better fit. No claimed CEFR or measured English gains.
Where do students stay and how do they arrive?
The 2026 sample used the student dormitory at California State University, East Bay (CSUEB). Meeting and dismissal were at San Francisco International Airport (SFO). 2027 logistics will be confirmed with the final schedule.
How does pricing work for agents vs parents?
2027 pricing is not published. Agents and schools: contact for current commercial discussion. Parents: guidance when the approved offer is ready. Historical fees are unresolved and are not a 2027 quote.
Who operates the program?
Delivered in partnership — SKYUS × Growing IQ (roles subject to written scope for each departure). Inquiry contact: matt@skyus.sg.
